# 11 — Owner ratification packets

Generated by `tools/reqaudit/render_ratification_packets.py`; do not edit by hand.

**Why this exists.** The mission recommended that the owner ratify the agent-recommended resolutions *by domain* rather than as a whole. When asked to 'do what you recommend', the five specific decisions OD-M-01..05 were recorded (data/owner-decisions.jsonl) but blanket ratification of the remaining resolutions was deliberately **not** recorded on the owner's behalf: approval should follow an actual review. Each packet below lists what the owner is approving.

**How to ratify.** Tell the agent, per packet: *ratify*, *ratify except <IDs>*, or *reject <IDs> with reason*. The agent then appends one `approved` (or `reopened`) event per question to `requirements/questions/resolution-events.jsonl`, re-runs `tools/reqaudit/adopt_convergence.py`, and the baseline index shows the clause as owner-approved. Ratification approves product direction only; it is not a legal, accounting, bank or auditor approval, and activation gates remain.
