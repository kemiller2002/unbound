Date: 2026-11-23 | Machine-readable: [`data/conflicts.csv`](data/conflicts.csv). Edges: `conflicts_with` in [`data/trace-edges.csv`](data/trace-edges.csv).

## 1. Method

A conflict is recorded only where two requirements cannot both be satisfied as written. Wording differences and duplicates are recorded separately as `necessity` findings titled "Duplicate:" in [02](02-QUALITY-AND-COVERAGE-REPORT.md). For each conflict the register records the incompatibility, the objective behind each side, alternatives, the least complex valid resolution, tradeoffs, and whether human authority is required. Prior-program contradictions (`TVC-*`) are cited where they remain open rather than being re-recorded.
