## 2. The most significant missing requirements

1. **Taxable-distribution recipient classes** (`UNB-RCN-GRT-001`): no requirement classified recipients into excepted public charities, expenditure-responsibility-required, or prohibited classes. This is the core of §4966.
2. **Related-party payee block** (`UNB-RCN-GRT-002`): automatic excess benefit under §4958(c)(2) was unguarded.
3. **Sanctions Blocked outcome and OFAC reporting** (`UNB-RCN-GRT-006/007`, `UNB-RCN-REG-003`): a true match had nowhere to go except return or retry paths.
4. **Assets never return to donors** (`UNB-RCN-FIN-013`): "refund" paths existed for accepted assets.
5. **Acknowledgment content, Form 8283/8282 and §4943 holdings** (`UNB-RCN-FIN-001..004`, `RR-UNB-ACC-006-r1`).
6. **Per-fund balancing and in-transaction capacity** (`UNB-RCN-FIN-006/009`): the ledger could move value between funds, or overcommit, without failing a check.
7. **Explicit funding states** (`UNB-RCN-FIN-008`) between reservation and payment.
8. **Unknown-payment resolution, check lifecycle and destination evidence** (`UNB-RCN-GRT-005/008/017`).
9. **Death suspends authority immediately** (`UNB-RCN-ENT-004`), before succession activation.
10. **Dormancy, closure, outbound transfer, fund split and merge** (`UNB-RCN-ENT-005..008`).
11. **Account-type classification and Schedule D package** (`UNB-RCN-ENT-011/012`).
12. **Tenant model and isolation map** (`UNB-RCN-PLT-001`), **global hold watermark** (`UNB-RCN-PLT-003`), **single authority matrix** (`UNB-RCN-PLT-005`).
13. **Requirements gate record and release-1 scope** (`UNB-RCN-ENT-001/002`).
14. **Donor-facing charity search** (`UNB-RCN-PLT-014`), the one unintentional market-baseline gap.
15. **Regulatory-change watch** (`UNB-RCN-REG-001`), **contract source of truth** (`UNB-RCN-ARC-001`), **terminology resolver** (`UNB-RCN-ARC-002`).

## 3. Consolidations made during integration

- `UNB-RCN-REG-004` (§4943 holdings) was withdrawn as a duplicate of `UNB-RCN-FIN-004`. Its finding (RCF-REG-010) now points to FIN-004.
