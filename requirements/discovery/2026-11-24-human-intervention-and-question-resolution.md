# Human intervention and question resolution

Status: proposed requirements, pending reconciliation with original records. Source: [question register](../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md). No production authorization or implementation.

## Proposed cross-cutting requirement: justified human intervention

Every mandatory human workflow step shall identify its legal, fiduciary, independent-control, uncertainty-resolution or measured economic justification, accountable owner, evidence, review date and safe automation alternative. Historical convention alone is insufficient. AI may prepare evidence and recommendations; deterministic controls and authorized principals govern financial execution. Removal of a control requires independent review and versioned approval. Measure total cost, error rates, harm and exception backlog before and after changes. Acceptance: every mandatory human step has a justification record; missing or expired justification triggers review, not automatic bypass; reserved decisions cannot be automated without recorded delegation.
