# 03: Missing requirements register (deliverable 3)

This register lists newly discovered capabilities, prioritized by importance and applicability. Each `UNB-XIN-*` record in `data/gap-requirements.jsonl` carries every mission §11 field (gate D2): discovery source, industry analogy, parent capability, failure conditions, security, AI, UI, API, reporting and testing implications. Each record also carries the §12 challenge: the argument for, the argument against, an existing alternative, cost and hidden complexity, customer friction, platform vs integration, and the simplest valid alternative. Every record has at least two Given/When/Then acceptance criteria (G6) and is tested by at least one scenario (D7).

## Priority scheme (mission §14)

| Priority | Meaning |
|---|---|
| P0 | Legal compliance, financial integrity, authorization, security, prevention of material harm |
| P1 | Essential end-to-end business operations and customer obligations |
| P2 | Significant automation, efficiency, scale and customer-experience improvements |
| P3 | Competitive differentiation and optional business-model expansion |
| P4 | Speculative opportunities that need more evidence |

Each record carries a one-sentence `priority_justification`.

**Priorities were revised by the adversarial review.** The research passes assigned priorities independently. Together they proposed 40 new P0s, plus 11 P0 refinements, which is not a credible release-1 set (XIF-ADV-003). The adversarial review challenged 48 priorities, and the integrator **applied** every numeric change (mission §16: "do not protect earlier conclusions"). Each changed record keeps the research pass's value in `priority_as_proposed`, and the reason in `priority_revision`. Seven records also carry an `adversarial_disposition` recommending rejection or merger (XRJ-ADV-001..007). They stay in the register, visibly marked, until the owner decides. The full list of challenges is `data/priority-challenges.jsonl`, rendered in [14](14-ADVERSARIAL-REVIEW.md).

Six P0s are conditional on what is in scope for release 1: card intake, ACH debit intake, direct foreign grants, call recording and pooled investment options. If those features are excluded, about six more P0s fall away (XID-ADV-001). Four requirements are written as if open decisions were already settled (XIF-ADV-019); they must follow those decisions.

**Decisions applied (2026-11-24).** Statements affected by the 41 legal and accounting decisions now carry the decided rule. Their prior wording is kept in `statement_as_proposed` ([16](16-LEGAL-AND-ACCOUNTING-DECISIONS.md)).
