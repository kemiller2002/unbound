**What the results say (inferences from the model, not observations).**
- **Shocks are paid in latency and exposure, not dollars.** Paired cost deltas are within ±$7/acct for 17 of 21 scenarios at 100k (the exceptions change the account count) because staffing is step-fixed and surge capacity is capped; among those 17, SLA attainment falls by up to 23 points (simultaneous failures) and some leave residual backlog. Cost-per-account dashboards would not see most operational failures (supports UNB-XIN-ECO-016 counter-metrics; RR-UNB-ECO-005-r1).
- **Growth looks like efficiency.** sudden_growth, major_sponsor_onboarding and major_migration *reduce* cost/acct (P50 Δ -48 for migration at 100k) while SLA attainment falls to 22% and 67,488 items remain unresolved 60 days after year-end (UNB-SMP-ECN-002).
- **Sponsor termination raises unit cost** by $24 (100k) / $14 (1M) — stranded step-fixed cost.
- **December is the binding staffing event** even with 50% seasonal staff: normal-year exception p90 wait ≈ 7.6 days vs a 5-day SLA; at k = 5 it is 14.1 days (cf. unbound:research/daf-theoretical-enterprise-validation/15-economics/economic-model.md:70-78).
- **Exposure is dominated by low-probability kinds with assumed parameters**: market drawdown over-commitment ($105m gross / $10.5m uncontained at 100k, wholly from the assumed 2% × 30% parameters), AI error (uncontained +$0.57m at 100k; scales with daily grant flow), cyber, recon. The control proxy *rises* in vendor-outage scenarios because large at-risk sums are contained — it must be read by kind, never as one KPI.
- **AI outage costs little money but measurable service**: +$0.7/acct, SLA -6 pts; incorrect AI outputs cost nothing visible in operating cost and appear only as uncontained exposure and post-payment reviews.

## Which assumptions matter most

*Source pass: Economic engineering and simulation.*

1. **Human minutes per exception × exception share (after determination reuse)** — top-3 in every full-service configuration (tornado swing 51% / 39% at OM1 100k). Unmeasured (TVA-002, TVA-003; GAP-001 per daf-landscape@6b3083a:research/ai-native-daf-enterprise/09-financial-models/financial-and-staffing-models.md:13). **Measure first**: shadow replay of a partner's grant history with timed reviews and recipient clustering for reuse.
2. **Loaded cost per FTE** — ρ ≈ +0.35 to +0.51 everywhere; the labour rate is unreconciled ($38.87–$86/h, TVC-020 via svc.md:161; unbound:research/daf-theoretical-enterprise-validation/15-economics/economic-model.md:96 GAP-040).
3. **Complex-asset share × specialist hours** — top-3 for a market book with HNW/institutional segments (OM1 tornado ranks 2–3). Outsourcing to vendors moves cost to funds (fund-borne $350/gift [A]) — a bearer change, not elimination.
4. **Accounts per sponsor and sponsor-level cost** — the **#1 driver in every platform model** (tornado swing 105% at OM3 1M, 97% at OM4 1M). Not previously a parameter in the Unbound or 09 models. Sponsor fragmentation, not account count, sets the platform floor.
5. **December multiplier k and contractor lead time** — determine SLA attainment, not cost (k unproven, TVA-004).
6. **Grants per account** — linear on exception work; 6.2–13 observed range [S].
7. **Human contacts per account** — matters only for DigitalMinimal books (top-10 in OM4); immaterial for full-service books (consistent with daf-landscape@6b3083a:research/ai-native-daf-enterprise/09-financial-models/financial-and-staffing-models.md:110 5.5%).

**Do not matter much (within stated ranges):** AI inference price, infrastructure (except at the $10–25 floor where infra_per_account is top-4), payment rails, custody bps (fund-borne anyway), audit, data providers.

**Falsification tests.** The model's main conclusions flip if (a) measured exception-minutes-per-grant at a lean sponsor exceed ~12 (then $258 fails even for OM3 at 1M; executed bisection `targets.258.OM3_1M_max_exception_minutes_per_grant` = 12.4 min/grant) or are below ~2 (then $100 becomes reachable for full-service at 1M; bisection = 1.97); (b) real sponsors on a shared platform average ≥25k accounts (sponsor-level cost falls below $5/acct); (c) measured contact rates for small accounts exceed ~0.1/yr (DigitalMinimal $25 fails).

**Independence caveat.** Model, parameters and this interpretation were produced by one model family in one session; the replication of the 09 numbers checks arithmetic, not truth. None of this is independent validation (UNB-ECO-006).
