## Scope, method and limitations

*Source pass: Friction, configuration and dashboards.*

**Group cx — phases Seven (customer friction, DOM CXP), Eight (configuration, DOM CFG) and Nine (dashboards, DOM DSH).** The analysis is requirements-level only. No code was written or executed against Unbound. The data files were generated by Python scripts and every line was checked to parse. Referenced IDs were checked against an index built from the unbound reconciliation and discovery data files, including inventory.csv.

|---|---|

**Sources read:**
- Unbound baseline: ADM, AIN, UX, INT, UNB-UX, and the 19-questions specifications on operational intelligence, work queues, the shared dashboard framework, AI-discoverable contracts and sponsor configuration.
- Reconciliation: reports 10 and 13; UI requirements UNB-UI-*; gap requirements UNB-RCN-*; decisions RCD-*.
- Discovery: reports 11, 14 and 17; UNB-XIN-* requirements; findings.
- daf-landscape: `docs/sponsor-white-label-grant-policy-affirmations-v0.1.md` and `docs/daf-enterprise-ui-requirements-v0.1.md`, both at working-tree HEAD 14c954a.

**Evidence labels:**
- All effort seconds are **[A]** analyst assumptions: active customer effort per occurrence, not measured. No usability study, contact data or analytics exist for Unbound.
- Counts of interactions, configuration items and metrics are **[D]**, derived by counting the generated files.
- Thresholds marked [A] are illustrative.

**Limitations:**
- One model produced every judgement here. This is not independent validation.
- The legal characterisations (§170(f)(18), Reg E, Nacha, §4966/§4967) are taken from the requirement texts that cite them. Where noted, they are `unverified — general knowledge`; statute text was not re-read.
- The task cites "reconciliation report 13" for Forma/Folio. Report 13 is the architecture dependency analysis, which holds the Forma/Folio verification matrix. Report 10 holds the UI Forma/Folio gap analysis. Both were used.

**Donor.**
- *Journeys:* open a fund, add money by ACH, give securities, recommend a grant, set up a recurring plan, add participants, ongoing servicing.
- *Removed:*
  - source-of-funds questionnaire (SPX-003; bank CDD rejected by XRJ-BNK-001);
  - micro-deposit round trip, replaced by instant validation, which UNB-XIN-PAY-002 already permits (SPX-011);
  - the standalone pledge question (SPX-026);
  - per-grant step-up for recommendations (SPX-027; XID-ADV-009);
  - annual affiliation re-attestation for most funds (SPX-037; XIF-ADV-018);
  - eligibility information requests that registry enrichment can answer (SPX-030).
- *Defaulted:*
  - funding allocation, purpose, recognition and communication preferences;
  - per-occurrence re-affirmation of recurring grants, replaced by a standing affirmation with event triggers (SPX-032; WL-13).
- *Highest-value change:* one grouped affirmation step on every grant (SPX-024; SPF-CXP-001).
- *Abandonment drivers [inference]:*
  - the document-and-selfie step (SPX-002);
  - the multi-day micro-deposit gap (SPX-011);
  - legal text density in affirmations;
  - re-entry when a session expires at review (UXA-4).
- *Repeat-contact drivers [inference, no contact data]:* the status questions "was my grant paid?", "where is my acknowledgment?" and "is my fund open?" (SPX-009, SPX-014, SPX-029, SPX-039). They become proactive catalog notices (SPF-CXP-003).
- *Phone-only donors:* the registered-device rule (UNB-XIN-SVC-004) blocks account-summary answers by phone (SPX-036). Keep the security goal but allow OTP to a verified channel for the account-summary class (XIF-ADV-018).

**Financial advisor.**
- *Journeys:* join a client fund; service many clients.
- *Removed or reduced:*
  - repeated identity proofing, by reusing firm-asserted identity where the firm contract carries assurance obligations (SPX-045);
  - firm-change re-entry, inferred from the HR/SCIM feed under UNB-XIN-CRM-007 (SPX-050);
  - manual per-client exports, replaced by a consented feed (SPX-052).
- *Retained:*
  - explicit role acceptance;
  - the clear client-context switch (wrong-client risk, TVC-011);
  - the acting human's own affirmation. AI never affirms.
- *Repeat-interaction driver:* co-advisor approvals for every grant. Use the any-one threshold under XID-ADV-004 (SPX-049).

**Charity.**
- *Removed:*
  - registration before the first grant (SPX-053; RR-UNB-GRANT-026-r1);
  - grantee receipt acknowledgment (SPX-056);
  - repeated vetting questionnaires per sponsor, since common evidence is reused as input under RCD-006 (SPX-061).
- *Retained:* destination enrolment and change controls (security) and supporting-organisation certification (law). Certifications are reused within their validity window.
- *Repeat-contact driver:* matching deposits to donors. Fix with structured remittance carrying the grant ID (REQ-X-077).

**Sponsor (officer).**
- *Dominant cost:* populating about 20 registers (SPX-064; XIF-ADV-004).
- *Change:* outcome questionnaire plus attestation of reviewed packs (Phase Eight).
- *Retained:* contract, board delegation, counsel approval of disclosure text, the A4 activation, and the opening-balance tie-out.

**Administrators.**
- *Eliminated:* manual impact assessment of policy changes, now simulated (SPX-072); human batch approval where the board has delegated routine approval to deterministic policy (SPX-075; RCD-005); cost thresholds, deferred (SPX-077).
- *Retained:* independent approval of policy changes.

**Employees.**
- *Eliminated:* the separate statement approval after close certification, pending RCD-038 (SPX-081).
- *Changed:* case summaries are machine-drafted and labelled under UNB-XIN-SVC-009 (SPX-082); attachments are extracted as Observations (SPX-083).
- *Retained:* sampled attestation (RCD-020) and verified-channel confirmation of assisted changes.

**Institutional partners.**
- *Changed:* default subscription sets (SPX-086); delivery calendars derived from provider profiles (SPX-087); migration mapping instead of donors re-entering data (SPX-088); default destinations with elect-out on partner exit (SPX-089).
- *Retained:* application registration, per-sponsor enablement and family-office consent.

**Totals [D from A]:** 483,515 s before and 73,128 s after across all listed steps. This sum is dominated by sponsor onboarding. It is not a customer-effort KPI. The KPI is defined in "Friction metrics".

**Not elimination.** No step was removed by moving its work to donors, advisors, charities or vendors. SPR-CXP-004 rejects one such idea.

**Rules that are never relaxed:**
- **Consent is never inferred** from silence, a pre-checked box, AI or unrelated activity (WL-06; UNB-RCN-GRT-010; SPR-CXP-001).
- **Separate consents stay separate:** a recurring-debit mandate is not a grant affirmation (WL-21). Affirmation evidence is per representation even when the UI groups the statements (SPF-CXP-001).
- **§170(f)(18).** The statutory element is the sponsor-issued acknowledgment stating the sponsor's exclusive legal control (UNB-RCN-FIN-001). It is retained and delivered proactively. The donor's acknowledgment of sponsor control at fund opening (SPX-006) is contract formation and donor understanding, so it is asked once per agreement version and never per contribution (SPF-CXP-007; counsel to confirm).
- **Legally required debit authorization** (Reg E/Nacha per UNB-XIN-PAY-001) is retained (SPX-012).
- **Donor control** is retained: amount, charity, successor shares, closure and transfer, reconsideration (SPX-020, SPX-034, SPX-040, SPX-043). These are the donor's advisory privileges. The sponsor retains legal control (IRC §4966(d)(2)).
- **Security step-up** is retained for authenticator, contact, payee and authority changes (XID-ADV-008, XID-ADV-009) and for workforce access. A passkey path must exist (WCAG 3.3.8; UNB-UI-A-005).

## Friction metrics

*Source pass: Friction, configuration and dashboards.*

These definitions are proposed in RR-ADM-042-r2. They extend RR-ADM-042-r1, and all of them live in the single metric registry (RR-REQ-X-043-r1).

| Metric | Definition | Numerator / denominator | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer effort per journey | Active seconds on required interaction steps to complete a journey | Sum of instrumented step durations; median and p90 per journey | Needs step instrumentation. Reported as "insufficient coverage" when coverage is below 95% [A] |
| Abandonment by step | Share of journeys that enter a step and do not complete within 7 days | Journeys entering step s without completion in 7 days / journeys entering s | Points to the exact step, as ADM-047 asks |
| Repeat interaction rate | Completed journeys in which the same fact was requested more than once from the same principal | Journeys with a repeated ask / completed journeys | Uses the RR-AIN-007-r1 source log |
| Repeat-contact rate | Same principal and reason within 7 days (RR-ADM-042-r1) | — | Already defined |
| Resolution time | First contact or submission to outcome communicated | Distribution by case kind | A case closes only when the outcome is communicated (RR-REQ-X-048-r2) |
| Unnecessary data entry rate | Facts asked that were held or retrievable at the time | Asked-but-held-or-retrievable / asked | Uses the source log. Target trend is toward 0 |
| Contacts per 1,000 accounts by reason | RR-ADM-042-r1 | — | Status reasons are the main elimination target |
| Deflection | RR-ADM-042-r1, always shown with repeat contacts | — | Deflection without resolution is not success |

**Guardrails:**
- No individual staff ranking (SPR-DSH-003).
- Effort surveys are optional and always shown with their response rate.
- Telemetry carries no sensitive values (daf-landscape UI product rules).
- Each friction metric is paired with a safety counter-metric so that friction is never reduced by weakening a control: affirmation-evidence completeness (SPM-035) and the false-hold rate (SPM-033).
