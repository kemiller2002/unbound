## Human-intervention taxonomy

*Source pass: Work inventory, operating model and staffing.*

**Rule zero: AI involvement alone never triggers human review.** A human touch is justified only by one of the nine classes below, evaluated on the *decision and evidence*, not on which component produced the evidence. A rule that would route "because AI was used" or "because AI confidence < x" must instead cite the evidentiary condition (normally Ambiguous evidence or Novel circumstances) — enforced at rule publication by UNB-SMP-ORG-001.

| # | Class | Definition | Membership test (all must hold) | Examples (SPW) | Existing requirement IDs | Can volume be reduced? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Legally required judgment or authorization | Law or regulation places the judgment, signature or liability on a person | A statute/regulation/binding guidance names a person, officer or qualified professional for this act; counsel-recorded | SPW-053 ED (qualified practitioner), SPW-054 ER, SPW-085 sanctions true-match, SPW-090 excise incidents, SPW-027 8283 signature, SPW-095 filing signature | UNB-XIN-NPG-001, UNB-RCN-GRT-003, CTL-013, UNB-XIN-NPG-008, UNB-RCN-FIN-002 | Only by changing the route (intermediaries, fewer foreign grants) |
| 2 | Fiduciary or governance responsibility | Sponsor's exclusive legal control or board duty requires a human decision | The decision changes authority, policy, risk appetite, investment policy, or is an exception to policy (org §3) | SPW-121 policy/DoA, SPW-061 discretionary holds, SPW-034 manager oversight, SPW-106 program activation, SPW-139 coverage gaps | UNB-GOV-001, UNB-GOV-002, UNB-XIN-NPG-006, UNB-RCN-WF-009 | Fewer, more general policies; default packs |
| 3 | Independent control function | A control requires a person independent of the doer | A control objective is independence (SoD pair, independent review, sampling) | SPW-082 breaks, SPW-088 sampling, SPW-133 IA, SPW-064 reconsideration, SPW-105 publisher | TVR-ORG-003, UNB-XIN-CLM-005, OPS-018, UNB-RCN-WF-018, CTL-006 | Sample sizes by risk; fix break sources |
| 4 | Material financial risk | Exposure above a configured threshold where error is costly or irreversible | Amount or exposure ≥ threshold in DoA **and** action irreversible or loss-bearing | SPW-071 NotReceived, SPW-062 shortfall beyond rule, SPW-023 return shortfall, SPW-063 recall after dispatch | UNB-RCN-WF-022, UNB-RCN-WF-026, UNB-XIN-ERP-005 | Thresholds tuned by measured loss, not comfort |
| 5 | Ambiguous evidence | Evidence is missing, contradictory or below identity threshold after bounded automated gathering | Deterministic rule cannot decide **and** automated evidence acquisition exhausted | SPW-049 identity merges, SPW-051 contradictory claims, SPW-021 unmatched lots, SPW-019 unmatched receipts | UNB-CHAR-005, RR-REQ-X-007-r3, UNB-XIN-CLM-003 | Yes — better sources, intent-first data capture |
| 6 | Novel circumstances | No rule exists for the situation | No published rule matches the case type, or the situation is flagged as first-of-kind | SPW-026 unusual assets, SPW-012 transfer to unknown sponsor, SPW-016 unclassified advisor relationship | UNB-WF-003, UNB-RCN-ENT-007, UNB-RCN-FIN-010 | Yes — each novel case should end in a rule or an explicit decline policy |
| 7 | Customer-requested assistance | A donor, advisor or charity asks for a person, complains, or needs an accommodation | Request recorded as a Contact promoted to a case (RR-REQ-X-048-r2 cause e) | SPW-098 (on request), SPW-099 complaints, SPW-102 accommodations, SPW-103 stewardship | UNB-XIN-SVC-001, UNB-XIN-SVC-005, UNB-GAP-026 | Not by refusal; by proactive status and better self-service |
| 8 | System failure | Automation, provider or data feed failed | An incident, Unknown or outage state exists | SPW-041 custodian outage, SPW-138 degraded mode, SPW-118 incidents, SPW-071 Unknown | REQ-X-006, TVR-OPS-002, UNB-RCN-GRT-008 | Yes — reliability engineering; measure per incident (TVR-ECON-003) |
| 9 | Avoidable administrative work | Human work caused by design gaps, re-keying, missing data or unclear rules | None of classes 1–8 applies | SPW-020 checks, SPW-046 missing info, SPW-024 intent chasing (conventional), SPW-017 manual billing (conventional) | UNB-RCN-PLT-006, REQ-X-042 | **Must** reduce: volume above threshold opens a prevention proposal (UNB-SMP-ORG-001) |

**Classification order.** Evaluate 1 → 9 and take the first match as primary class; secondary classes may be recorded. Classes 1–3 never disappear (they can only be re-routed); classes 4–6 shrink with better rules and data; class 7 shrinks with proactive service but must always remain available; class 8 shrinks with reliability; class 9 should trend to zero.

**Mapping to authority scale.** Classes 1–2 are A4/A5 in the authority matrix (UNB-RCN-PLT-005); class 3 is an independence requirement on whoever decides; class 4 maps to DoA monetary limits (UNB-XIN-ERP-005).

## Assignment models

*Source pass: Work inventory, operating model and staffing.*

Assignment is the selection of a person **within the eligible set**; it never confers authority (REQ-X-048; RR-REQ-X-048-r1). RR-REQ-X-048-r3 fixes precedence: eligibility (DoA for the action class, skill, sponsor scope, availability, conflicts, SoD history) is computed first; the mode chooses within it; an empty set falls through a declared chain ending in escalation.

| Model | Right when | Wrong when | Configuration | Failure behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Round robin | Homogeneous, short, low-risk tasks with interchangeable staff (e.g. information-request follow-ups, document indexing) | Variable complexity (unfair, ADV-639), relationship continuity matters, specialist skills needed | Durable cursor per queue × sponsor; eligible roster snapshot per assignment | Cursor loss → recompute from audit log; concurrent intake must not double-assign (ADV-619) |
| Specialization (skill-based) | ED/ER, complex assets, foreign grants, sanctions, fraud, accessibility, language | Volume too low to keep specialists busy (pool them across sponsors or fractional) | Skill tags on worker and task; minimum two qualified per skill (coverage) | No qualified eligible → next fallback (other sponsor pool if permitted, then escalation) |
| Relationship ownership (sticky) | Advisor-distributed, HNW and institutional sponsors; service continuity | Owner absence; conflicts with the payee/donor; independence required | OwnershipBinding by sponsor/fund/advisor book with backup and inheritance | Owner unavailable → backup → pool; ownership itself unchanged unless departed |
| Cradle-to-grave | Grants needing several human steps; succession; complex gifts; complaints | Simple single-step items (adds coordination cost) | One case owner from creation to closure; specialist tasks return to owner | Owner departs → reassign with history; owner can never be the independent approver of own case |
| Escalation | Time, amount, cumulative-limit or coverage triggers; fraud and sanctions (escalation-only routing) | As a first resort for routine work | Tiered authority levels with SLA timers; cumulative DoA aggregation (UNB-XIN-ERP-005) | Top tier unavailable → board-designated alternate; item stays held, never auto-approved |
| Reassignment | Absence beyond threshold, departure/DoA expiry, new conflict, SLA risk, specialist completion | As a supervisor convenience without reason | Triggers and thresholds in AssignmentPolicy; manual override with reason | Must not select SoD-ineligible person; reported per supervisor |
| Workload balancing (capacity-weighted) | Pools with varied complexity and capacity; December surge | Tiny teams (overhead exceeds benefit) | Effort-weighted units from time equations (UNB-XIN-ECO-006); capacity from schedules | Stale capacity data → fall back to round robin within eligible set; never rank individuals by raw handle time (svc.md measurement rules) |

**Default policy pack (proposed; SPD-ORG-005).**

| Case kind | Primary | Fallback chain | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exception grant decision (A4) | Skill-based (capacity-weighted within skill) | Other qualified pool → Escalation to compliance lead → accountable executive | Decider ≠ preparer/originator |
| Grant case coordination | Cradle-to-grave | Backup owner → pool | Only where >1 human step |
| Charity vetting exception | Skill-based (shared registry pool) | Sponsor compliance → Escalation | Sponsor-specific decisions stay in sponsor scope |
| Payee change / destination exception | Skill-based (treasury) | Escalation | Approver ≠ releaser |

| Sanctions potential match | Escalation-only to sanctions decider | Alternate decider | ≠ grant approver |
| Fraud / restricted investigation | Escalation-only | Counsel | Restricted access class |
| Service request / complaint | Sticky relationship (advisor/HNW sponsors) else round robin | Pool → supervisor | Complaint closure needs response + root cause |
| Succession | Cradle-to-grave | Backup | Human document evaluation |
| Complex gift | Cradle-to-grave + specialist | Committee | Acceptance authority |
| Reconciliation break | Skill-based (independent reconciler) | Escalation to finance authority (not releaser) | Independence |

## Escalation models

*Source pass: Work inventory, operating model and staffing.*

| Trigger type | Rule | From → to | Timer default [A] | Never |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier escalation (decision routing) | Deterministic → bounded AI evidence → eligible human → second-level authority (REQ-X-017) | Gate → case owner → decider → senior decider | per case profile | AI deciding; fail-open |
| Amount / cumulative limit | Item > per-item DoA limit or approver's cumulative total for aggregation key exceeded (UNB-XIN-ERP-005) | Decider → next DoA level | immediate | Splitting to evade limits (aggregation key) |
| SLA / aging | Item approaching profile clock | Assignee → fallback → supervisor → accountable executive | 50% / 90% / 100% of clock | Auto-approval on timeout (organization §3 TVC-007 issue) |
| No eligible assignee | Empty eligible set (RR-REQ-X-048-r3) | Fallback chain → named authority | 1 business hour | Assigning an ineligible person |
| Coverage gap | Coverage roster fails (UNB-SMP-ORG-002) | Roster → accountable executive → board for emergency delegate | ≥10 business days ahead | Relaxing SoD |
| Risk / adverse signal | Sanctions potential match, fraud signal, adverse charity event | Direct to sanctions/fraud decider (escalation-only routing) | immediate | Routing through general service queue |
| Legal | Possible taxable distribution, regulator inquiry, privileged matter | Counsel; committee within configured period (UNB-XIN-NPG-008) | configured | Staff self-determining liability |
| System failure | Unknown state, outage, feed stale | Ops → treasury pair → executive for degraded mode (two persons, TVR-OPS-002) | per RTO | Retrying Unknown blindly |
| Customer | Request for a person, complaint, regulator complaint | AI/self-service → case owner → complaint handler → counsel (regulator) | 2 / 15 business days (svc.md §4) | Closing before customer informed (RR-REQ-X-048-r2) |
| Recurring exception | Class-9 volume above threshold two periods (UNB-SMP-ORG-001) | Rule owner → REQ-X-042 proposal → policy author/publisher | period close | Silent tolerance |
