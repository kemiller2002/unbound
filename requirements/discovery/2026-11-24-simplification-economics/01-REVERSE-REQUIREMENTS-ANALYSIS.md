**Comparison with industry evidence.** Industry process pools: routine eligibility verification ~$139k per 1M grants ($0.14/grant), marked "should not exist per grant" [S daf-landscape:research/mission-2026-11-14/ai-economics-replacement-analysis.md:56]; non-standard diligence ~$1.25M per 1M grants [S daf-landscape:research/mission-2026-11-14/ai-economics-replacement-analysis.md:57; unbound:requirements/discovery/2026-11-23-cross-industry/industry/grt.md:206-207]. AI's best fit is evidence assembly on the non-standard residual, after redesign [S daf-landscape:research/mission-2026-11-14/ai-economics-replacement-analysis.md:127]. Our target residual excludes external ED/ER fees and R1 excludes direct foreign/ER grants (RCD-014; report 17), so the target is not comparable to the full P07 pool.
