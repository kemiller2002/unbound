## Policy packs, explainable defaults, conflict detection, configuration simulation and controlled overrides

*Source pass: Friction, configuration and dashboards.*

Requirements-level design for UNB-SMP-CFG-002, RR-ADM-027-r1, RR-ADM-048-r1 and UNB-SMP-CFG-003.

**Policy packs.** The packs are PK-LEGAL-US-DAF, PK-RAIL-US, PK-ACCT-NFP, PK-ESCHEAT-US, PK-SERVICE, PK-SECURITY, PK-TIMING, PK-RETENTION-US and PK-OPS.
- Each item carries: reviewing authority and date, legal basis, default rationale, and an override direction (tightenOnly, lengthenOnly, sponsorChoice or none).
- Packs are semantically versioned and published under dual control.
- A sponsor adopts a pack by an A4 attestation recording the pack hash.
- Transactions and issued documents pin the pack version in force.
- Upgrade propagation is SPD-CFG-001; liability is SPD-CFG-003.

**Explainable defaults.** Every default is displayed as "From PK-x vN: <rationale> (basis: <citation>)". The same text is in the assistance contract (RR-REQ-X-051-r2), so the UI, help text and AI cannot diverge.

**Controlled overrides.**
- A deterministic comparator per value type checks the override direction. Days and business days are distinct types.
- Loosening is rejected unless a recorded counsel deviation exists, with an owner and expiry.
- Overrides are counted (SPM-057) and pack currency is monitored (SPM-058).

**Conflict detection.**
- Declared conflict rules in the Configuration Register are evaluated together with platform hard controls and pack baselines.
- Precedence follows REQ-X-018: stricter applicable law and platform safety prevail, and a sponsor may only tighten.
- An unresolved conflict blocks approval.

**Configuration simulation.**
- Before approval, the change is simulated against open items: grants, schedules, holds, payment instructions, fee accruals and pending statements.
- The last 90 days of decisions [A] are replayed under the proposed version, and changed outcomes are reported.
- A breach of a platform-safety parameter blocks approval (UNB-RCN-WF-021).
- Simulation runs in tenant scope on synthetic or tenant data and never posts.

**Configuration is a cost (UNB-SMP-CFG-003).** A new setting cannot merge without a register entry and an approved disposition. Platform-owned and eliminated items are absent from sponsor APIs and UI.

| config/policy | dependsOn[], affects[] (contract IDs); conflictRules[] | Dependencies |

| config/policy | disposition (from the Configuration Register) | Whether it is exposed at all |

**AI behaviour:**
- An explanation must cite the contract version and legalBasis. If legalBasis is missing, the AI says the basis is undocumented and does not infer one.
- A surface whose contract lacks a required field is not assistant-ready, and AI help is hidden there.
- AI may draft diffs (L2). It never publishes, never authors legal values, and never changes NBA ranking (UNB-SMP-DSH-001).
