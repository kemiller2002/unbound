**When scale adds people and when it does not.**
- **Does not add people:** deterministic per-transaction work (approval of routine grants, eligibility checks with fresh evidence, acknowledgments, statements, postings, matching, fee computation) — zero marginal minutes; governance, executive, policy, BCP, tax filing, audit support — fixed per sponsor; platform roles — operator-side, step-wise.
- **Adds people linearly:** exception-driven authority roles — charitable compliance deciders (grants × e × m), gift acceptance (contributions × complex share × hours), service case owners (accounts × residual human-contact rate), treasury exceptions (payments × exception rate). At 100k accounts these are ≈82% of minimum-touch workload [D].
- **Adds people in steps:** partner/vendor management, security, engineering (S3), seasonal surge capacity.
- **Levers ranked (expected case):** exception share *e* and minutes *m* (each halving halves the compliance pool, economic-model.md:84-88); complex-gift hours (12 h baseline [A] vs ≈2 h minimum-touch [A] — the single most fragile assumption, SPF-ORG-006); December surge flattening by giving plans (TVA-030 unmeasured); proactive status for contacts.
- **Sensitivity check [D]:** if minimum-touch exception decisions keep the baseline e = 8% and m = 45 (e·m = 3.6 vs 0.9 assumed), SPW-060 alone rises from 7.5 to 30 FTE at 100k (÷1,600 h), and S1 staff from ≈41.3 to ≈69.4 FTE.

**Derivation of the minimum pool (payment chain, worst case item) [D].** The pair graph is bipartite, so 2 people satisfy all pairs except the three-distinct PA-2 rule (org §4:85). With per-item SoD evaluation against subject history (org §5.2:108), a cross-trained pool of *n* treasury-authorized holders can decide any item if ≥3 are available (or ≥2 under SPD-ORG-001). Coverage depth then sets the pool: **single-absence cover → n = 4; double-absence (year-end) → n = 5** (with the reconciler external). review-fin-ops B-2's ≈10+ counts six seats twice (unbound:research/daf-theoretical-enterprise-validation/13-adversarial-reviews/raw/review-fin-ops.md:209) and over-counts because the same person may hold several seats on different items (SPF-ORG-003; SPR-ORG-001).

**Practical sponsor floor (S1) [D/A].** 4–5 named authority holders (executive, finance authority, compliance lead, operations lead, + 1 cross-trained), board, and external independent reconciler, internal audit/sampling, counsel and CPA. Not reducible further without SPD-ORG-002. Workload, not SoD, determines FTE above ≈10k accounts.

**What does NOT require distinct people.** Preparing and coordinating a case (cradle-to-grave owner) and deciding a *different* case; holding both treasury and compliance authority on different items; executive also serving as policy author (but then not publisher of that version); one person approving payee changes for some payees and releasing payments to others.

## Staffing cost model

*Source pass: Work inventory, operating model and staffing.*

**Formulas.**
- Workload hours per role group *r*: W_r(A) = Σ_{i∈r} V_i(A) × m_i / 60, with V_i(A) = V_i(100k) × A/100k (linear drivers), V_i(100k) (fixed), V_i(100k) × (A/100k)^0.3 (step) [A].
- Workload FTE: F_r = W_r / (H × u); H = 1,600 h [A, economic-model.md:32], u = 0.80 [A, UNB-XIN-ECO-006 default].
- Authority floor persons: P_min = coverage-depth pool for the binding class (4 single / 5 double absence) [D]; floor FTE = P_min × s_min, s_min = 0.5 [A].
- Sponsor staff FTE = max(floor FTE, Σ_staff F_r); December peak heads = staff + Σ_{exception roles} F_r × (peak/avg − 1), peak/avg = 250k/(225+25k) = 2.5 at k = 3 [S, economic-model.md:61-67].
- Staff cost = FTE × C, C = $165k expected, $124.5k low, $250k high [A/S, economic-model.md:33].
- Exception-pool cost per account (economic-model form): 8·e·m/(60·H)·C [S formula, economic-model.md:45].
- Total people cost = staff cost + purchased services (counsel, IA/sampling, CPA, reconciliation review, PEO, fractional vCISO) [A].

## Limitations

*Source pass: Work inventory, operating model and staffing.*

- **No measurement.** All minutes, volumes and purchased-service amounts are assumptions; aggregates are arithmetic on assumptions. The two dominant parameters (exception minutes and complex-gift hours) are unmeasured (SPF-ORG-006).
- The conventional column is a same-scope comparison of direct activity time only; it is not a conventional headcount estimate.
- Legal conclusions are not made: delegation of routine approval (OD-01), SoD status of the deterministic gate (SPD-ORG-001), fractional officers (SPD-ORG-002) and cross-sponsor reliance are open counsel/governance decisions.
- Same-model analysis; no independent human review. Role-level authority mappings should be tested against a real sponsor's bylaws and DoA.
- The '~$258/account' figure cited in the mission brief was not found; $272 is the sourced modelled figure.
