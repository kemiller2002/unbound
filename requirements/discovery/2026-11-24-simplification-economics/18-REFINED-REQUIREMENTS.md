### UNB-SMP-ORG-001 (P1, proposed)

- **Statement:** Every WorkItem, human approval task and human touch recorded against an outcome MUST carry exactly one primary HumanInterventionClass from the versioned taxonomy {LegallyRequiredJudgment, FiduciaryGovernance, IndependentControl, MaterialFinancialRisk, AmbiguousEvidence, NovelCircumstance, CustomerRequestedAssistance, SystemFailure, AvoidableAdministrative}, assigned deterministically by the creating rule (not by the assignee), plus the rule id and the authority-matrix action class (UNB-RCN-PLT-005). 'AI involvement', 'AI confidence' or 'AI-generated' MUST NOT be a class or a sufficient creating condition; a rule that routes AI output to a human MUST cite one of the nine classes (normally AmbiguousEvidence or NovelCircumstance) with the evidence condition. Reporting MUST show human touches per 1,000 outcomes by class, rule and sponsor.
