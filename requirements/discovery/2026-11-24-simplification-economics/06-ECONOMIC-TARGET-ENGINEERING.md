**Other revenue forms.**
- **Manual processing fees** (check, wire, stop-payment, complex asset): acceptable only as disclosed, donor-affirmed, cost-based service charges with recovery ratio ≤1.10 (UNB-XIN-ECO-018, XID-ECO-002). The model shows check grants cost ~$4.50 vs $0.46 ACH; a fee that recovers that difference steers to ACH without profit. A fee that exceeds cost **creates an incentive to keep manual paths alive** — flag.
- **Platform fees / white-label**: same economics as institutional licensing; per-sponsor fixed component is needed because sponsor-level cost (~$37k platform + ~$60k entity per sponsor [A]) is the top tornado driver in platform models (SPD-ECN-003).
- **Bundled financial services** (investment management, model-portfolio sponsorship, sub-advisory 0–100 bps; DED:38-47): revenue that grows with retained assets and with product placement — **asset-retention and conflict incentive**; must be disclosed and separated from grant operations; profitability must never enter routing (UNB-XIN-ECO-012).
- **Float / interest on cash awaiting grant**: rewards slow grant payment — already rejected (XRJ-ECO-001).

**Models that encourage unnecessary work, asset retention or delayed grants:** per-grant transaction fees (delayed/withheld grants), asset-based fees and advisor/bundled fees (retention), above-cost manual processing fees (preserves manual work), float income (slow payment), outcome pricing per approved grant (unsafe approval; unbound:requirements/discovery/2026-11-23-cross-industry/11-OPERATING-ECONOMICS.md:14). The least-distorting combinations on this model are **(a) a declining asset fee with a transparent, disclosed cross-subsidy for a DigitalMinimal class, or (b) institutional licensing with separate per-sponsor and per-account components**; both still carry a retention incentive that can only be mitigated by disclosure and by payout-oriented product design, not eliminated.
