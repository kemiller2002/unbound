**Reading.**
- The **marginal cost of an additional account** is ~$0.30–3 theoretical and **$12–20/yr practical**; the practical figure is dominated by expected human contacts (0.3/yr × 20 min ≈ $10 at baseline) and recurring obligations ($3–6), not by technology. BENCH measured 2,127–3,944 tx/s on 4 vCPU [M] against ~200 tx/s needed at Fidelity-scale burst (unbound:requirements/discovery/2026-11-23-cross-industry/18-ARCHITECTURE-BENCHMARK.md:31-39, :47), so compute is not the binding cost.
- A **routine ACH grant** costs $0.46 theoretical; its practical $3–8 is almost entirely expected exception labour (8% × 45 min at baseline). Every point of exception rate is worth ~$1.4/grant at OM1 [D]. Determination reuse is the largest single lever (daf-landscape@6b3083a:research/ai-native-daf-enterprise/09-financial-models/financial-and-staffing-models.md:91; unbound:research/daf-theoretical-enterprise-validation/15-economics/economic-model.md:84).
- **Routine charity verification** is $0.14 [S] (unbound:requirements/discovery/2026-11-23-cross-industry/industry/grt.md:206) and is reusable across all grants to the same EIN; **non-standard (ED) verification** ~$4k [S] (grt.md:208) is ~$160/grant when amortised over ~12.5 grants per recipient over its ~2-year validity — the true floor depends on reuse, not on automation.
- **A sponsor** costs $5k–37k/yr to the platform plus ~$60k/yr of its own entity costs [A]; this is the cost that makes small sponsors uneconomic and that the tornado ranks first in platform models.
- **A service case** resolved by AI is ≤$0.50 (svc.md:172, illustrative); a human async case is ~$34 at modelled minutes. **A payment failure** is $5 automated vs ~$29 manual.

**Do minimum balances and fees reflect inherent cost or history?** On this model, **history and distribution, not inherent marginal cost.** The $100 (Fidelity, DED:66) and $250 (RCF, DED:65) minimum fees [S] are close to the *fully allocated* cost of a small account in a conventional/OM1 cost structure (OM1@100k small-recurring fully allocated $115) and above it under lean structures (OM3@1M $50; OM4 $28). The inherent *marginal* cost of a low-activity digital account is $5–24/yr. Minimums also encode non-cost factors the model does not capture: advisor compensation embedded in tiers (RCF advisor-managed 65 bps, DED:64), distribution economics, and acquisition (~$60/acct here). This is an **inference** from an assumption-driven model; it becomes evidence only when UNB-XIN-ECO-004/007 produce graded actuals by balance band.

## Worked examples

*Source pass: Economic engineering and simulation.*

WE-1 to WE-4 are deterministic (modal-input, noise-off) runs of the executed model; reproduce with `M.run_once(cfg, om, accounts, scenario, random.Random(0), noise=False)`.

**WE-1 Backlog arithmetic (OM3, 10,000 accounts, December peak k = 5).** Exception arrivals 2,870/yr; base rate = 2,870/(225+25·5) = 8.20/day; December = 41.0/day. Staffing ⌈annual minutes/(60·1,600)·1.15⌉ = 2 FTE + 50% seasonal; capacity per FTE/day = 6.4 h × 60 / 30 min = 12.8 → 38.4/day in December. Result: peak backlog 65, p90 wait 1.29 days, SLA attainment 100%. At 10k the 2-FTE floor gives slack; the same arithmetic at 100k (11 FTE, no floor slack) yields p90 wait 14.1 days (MC P50).

**WE-2 Simultaneous failures (OM3, 100,000 accounts).** k = 4, 25% staff absence days 225–239, vendor outage (0.02 unknown-state cases per account), service demand ×3, exception handle time ×2 during AI outage, ops arrivals ×5. Ops queue: 10,871 arrivals, peak backlog 6,507, SLA 28%, not cleared 60 days into the next year; service queue peak 6,989. Cost/acct $148.26 vs $147.00 normal; overall SLA 59%; gross exposure $65.5m of which $0.58m uncontained. Lesson: the 2-FTE ops floor with a 50%+1 contractor cap is the weakest point; ops needs a pre-contracted surge bench (SPD-ECN-004).

**WE-3 Incorrect AI outputs (OM3, 100,000 accounts).** Daily routine grant flow = annual grant dollars/250 = $16.62m; f = 1%, detection after 5 days ⇒ gross $0.831m paid in error (same form as unbound:research/daf-theoretical-enterprise-validation/15-economics/economic-model.md:106-108); recovery 60% [A] ⇒ uncontained $0.332m; potential §4966 excise on the taxable share 10% [A] × 20% [S] = $16,616. Post-payment review cases = grants/250 × 5 × 1% = 145. Operating cost unchanged; control proxy 0.871 vs 0.928. Detection time, not model price, is the lever.

**WE-4 DigitalMinimal unit economics (OM4, 1,000,000 accounts).** Accounts 15% inactive / 40% small recurring / 45% workplace. Modal cost $23.94/acct = account-level $11.82 + platform fixed $6.83 + sponsor-level $4.85 (+ identity). Staff 61 FTE ({'exceptions': 11, 'complex': 1, 'service': 10, 'ops': 9}); the ops queue (ACH returns on small-recurring debits plus reconciliation breaks, 15–20 min each at $1.719/min) is nearly as large as the exception queue, although contributions (16,515,000/yr) are mostly batched payroll items. Revenue: $9 (0.6% × $1.5k workplace) to $30 (small recurring $5k); employer $12 alone loses money. Viable only with ≥~$25/acct revenue or a disclosed cross-subsidy.

**WE-5 The $258 arithmetic.** ($508,000 × 12 / 25,000 + $27,200,000 / 100,000) / 2 = ($243.84 + $272.00) / 2 = $257.92 [D].
