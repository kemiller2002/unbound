# Open questions register

Every question in the requirements missions, joined to its recorded answer. Generated by `tools/reqaudit/render_questions.py`; do not edit by hand. Answers live in resolution records under `requirements/resolutions/` (see its README), keyed by a stable question key, so the same question keeps its answer when other questions change. [`open-questions.csv`](open-questions.csv) has every column.

**Answers are product directions.** An answer with an external gate still needs the named counsel, CPA, auditor, bank, board or counterparty before the capability is activated; a validation gate needs the stated test, pilot or measurement. Answering does not change a decision record's own status.
