# Open questions register

Every question in the requirements missions that still needs an answer, in one place. Generated by `tools/reqaudit/render_questions.py` from the mission data files and the resolution log; do not edit by hand. Answers, approvals, evidence dispositions and their history are recorded only in the append-only log [`questions/resolution-events.jsonl`](questions/resolution-events.jsonl) (see [`questions/README.md`](questions/README.md)); [`open-questions.csv`](open-questions.csv) is an output that joins each question to its current resolution. The tool refuses to regenerate if the CSV holds an answer the log does not.

**Three kinds of question:**

- **Open decisions** have not been decided. Each has a recommendation.
- **Awaiting confirmation** are decided provisionally and need the named professional or owner to confirm them.
- **Requirement questions** are open points on proposed requirements and refinements.

**Who answers** is grouped by keyword from each record's owner field, with counsel taking precedence. Unbound is a software platform only (SPD-ADV-004), so a question owned by a sponsor role usually means the product owner decides the default and the setting the sponsor can change.
