# Unbound first-principles requirements policy
Status: normative draft v0.1

## Primary rule
**Preserve the outcome, not the process.** Every inherited process is a hypothesis, not a requirement. Preserve only verified legal duties, legitimate business outcomes, safety, rights, accountability, and measurable controls.

## Design review protocol
For every candidate workflow:
1. Define the desired outcome and affected stakeholders.
2. Identify each purported obligation with jurisdiction, primary source, date and qualified-review status.
3. Classify each step: statutory/regulatory, fiduciary, contractual, sponsor policy, risk control, convention, or unnecessary.
4. Ask whether the activity, department, form, queue, approval or interface can be eliminated, combined, delegated, automated or replaced.
5. Generate at least three materially different solutions, including one that does not resemble the incumbent workflow.
6. Evaluate legality, control effectiveness, failure recovery, user agency, accessibility, time, cost, error risk, observability and reversibility.
7. Select a provisional design only with explicit evidence and documented tradeoffs. Do not mistake agent confidence for verification.
8. Attach acceptance criteria and adversarial tests. Route legal ambiguities and irreversible decisions to authorized humans.

## EF demonstration hypothesis
Hypothesis: Echelon Foundry can create and improve an enterprise more effectively than conventional methods. This is **not yet established**. Measure agent autonomy, human intervention, cost, elapsed time, defect rates, rework, evidence quality, and portability, including failed experiments.

## Hard boundaries
No real sponsor representation, tax advice, investment advice, asset custody, bank transfers, grant payments, or regulatory filings from this reference environment. Use synthetic identities and simulated transactions. Production claims require separate approvals and independent review.


## Mandatory SOC 2 evidence-by-design directive (2026-11-21)
All subsequent design, research, agent missions, implementation and operations MUST follow [SOC 2 Controls, Evidence and Enterprise Directive](../requirements/governance/SOC2-CONTROLS-EVIDENCE-AND-ENTERPRISE-DIRECTIVE.md) and [Sponsor Authority and Autonomous Operations](../requirements/governance/SPONSOR-AUTHORITY-AND-AUTONOMOUS-OPERATIONS.md). For every claimed control specify owner, scope, procedure, cadence, expected evidence, monitoring, exception handling, test and retention. Log security-relevant and consequential actions with attributable tamper-evident evidence while excluding secrets and unnecessary personal data. Include negative tests and report gaps truthfully. SOC 2 readiness is a design requirement, not a claim of completed attestation or operational compliance. No live outbound payment execution is authorized.
